04 — Workflow Three steps

Three steps, then it runs itself.

Step 01

Point it at the mess.

Connect Dropbox, Drive, S3 or the NAS in the corner. Tessera indexes in place — nothing moves until you say so.

Step 02

Let it read everything.

Subjects, scenes, colour, faces you hold releases for, and the words baked into layouts. Duplicates collapse on their own.

Step 03

Search instead of asking.

The question that used to be a message to the producer is now a sentence in a search field — answered in under a second.
